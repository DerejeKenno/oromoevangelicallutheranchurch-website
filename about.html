About Us

Serving God and community with faith and compassion.

Oromo Evangelical Lutheran Church exists to glorify God through worship, biblical teaching, prayer, fellowship, and practical service to others.

Who we are

We are a church family committed to the Gospel of Jesus Christ and to creating a place where individuals and families can grow in faith, experience community, and serve with purpose.

Located at 1761 Bairsford Dr, Columbus, OH 43232, our congregation welcomes people from all backgrounds to worship, pray, and grow together in Christ.

Core Values

  • Christ-centered worship
  • Biblical teaching
  • Prayer and spiritual growth
  • Compassionate service
  • Unity in community

Mission

To proclaim the Gospel, equip believers, and serve our neighbors through the love of Christ.

Vision

To be a spiritually vibrant church that impacts families, communities, and future generations.

Community

To foster belonging, mutual care, and faithful partnership in ministry.
