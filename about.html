About Us

Serving God and community with faith, compassion, and hope.

Elshaddai Oromo Evangelical Church exists to glorify God through worship, biblical teaching, prayer, fellowship, and practical service to others.

Who we are

We are a Christ-centered church family committed to the Gospel of Jesus Christ and to creating a place where individuals and families can worship faithfully, grow in God’s Word, experience genuine fellowship, and serve with purpose.

Located at 1761 Bairsford Dr, Columbus, OH 43232, our congregation warmly welcomes people from all backgrounds to gather for worship, prayer, discipleship, and shared life in Christ.

Core Values

  • Christ-centered worship
  • Biblical teaching
  • Prayer and spiritual growth
  • Compassionate service
  • Unity in community

Mission

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, equip believers for faithful living, and serve our neighbors through the love of Christ.

Vision

To be a spiritually vibrant church that strengthens families, blesses communities, and leaves a lasting legacy of faith for future generations.

Community

To foster belonging, mutual care, prayerful encouragement, and faithful partnership in ministry as one church family.
